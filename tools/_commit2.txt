Set the Google client id, and fix a silent empty sign-in button

- GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID in wrangler.toml and VITE_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID in the local .env.
  The two must match: the Worker checks the token's aud claim against its own copy,
  and a mismatch refuses every sign-in with no message the player can act on.
- renderGoogleButton returned true after an unchecked renderButton call. It can
  finish without painting - unsupported origin, blocked iframe, stale script - and
  the caller, seeing true, skipped its fallback and left an empty box. A blank area
  reads as "this game has no Google sign-in". It now waits frames for the button to
  appear and reports honestly when it did not.
- The "service not configured" notice named only VITE_ACCOUNT_API. Google sign-in
  needs the Worker too, so following it left you setting one variable and then
  wondering why no Google button appeared.
- Fixed a typo in two Vietnamese strings written through a patch script.
