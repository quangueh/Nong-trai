Make the sign-in gate a full screen rather than a corner sheet

It reused .overlay and .sheet, which is a bottom-anchored panel with rounded top
corners. Correct for a dialog you opened on purpose, wrong for the thing that greets you
at the gate: on a phone it filled one corner with the blurred garden beside it, which
read as a popup that could be dismissed rather than a door to come through.

Now full bleed with the card centred and the garden dimmed behind it.

Two measurements caught what the eye would have called fine. The overlay was absolute,
so it filled .shell rather than the viewport - and .shell is a centred column capped at
758px on a 1180px screen, leaving the page background showing either side of a gate.
`.gate-overlay` is fixed for that reason. The card also inherited the sheet's slide-up
animation, which implies arrival from a tap; this arrives from a page load, so it rises
instead.

Verified at 360x640, 390x844 and 1180x820: the overlay covers the viewport exactly, the
card is centred and fits, and nothing overflows the bottom.
