Shop
- Locked species are listed with the requirement attached instead of being
  filtered out. A shelf that omits what is locked cannot answer "what do I do
  next", which is the only reason anyone browses a catalogue this size. Tier chips
  are clickable when locked, and there is a filter for locked-only stock.
- The registry size is no longer printed. It invites "how many have I not seen"
  instead of the question the screen exists to answer.
- The x10 pack button is gated like the single seed. It checked only the balance,
  so a locked species showed a live button that accepted the tap and refused in
  silence.

Combat
- Eight elements, eight behaviours: one particle system, distributed differently
  per element (fire rises, water arcs back down, earth drops, shadow converges).
  Verified rather than eyeballed - diag-element-fx fails if any two share all
  three of direction class, vertical class and lopsidedness band.
- Particles got a dark rim. Light (#fff3c4) and electric (#ffe45e) were the same
  value as the arena behind them, so a light impact photographed as nothing.

Breeding
- Both parents are consumed. To keep that from being a pure tax, their growth
  passes to the child.
- The cost is named on screen before the button, including which two plants.
- Five protocols, each with an explicit downside, gated by breeder level. The
  odds shown are the odds the roll uses, checked by breeding 260 children per
  protocol and comparing.

Google sign-in
- ID token verified in the Worker against Google's published keys: alg, signature,
  kid, aud, iss, exp, email_verified. Exercised against real RS256 signatures.
- Google and email accounts do not merge, even on the same address.

Fixes found along the way
- The breeding meta block used the "divider" class, a 1px hairline, as a
  container. 70px of rows rendered inside a 1px box and spilled outside the card.
  Invisible until something sat below it.
- Protocol chips were disabled at every level: disabled: "" still sets the
  attribute.
- The cost notice never entered the DOM: Element.after() on a parentless node is a
  silent no-op.
- worker/src/index.ts had never been typechecked - its declared devDependency was
  not installed. Installing it surfaced a real signature mismatch in b64.
