A proper Google sign-in screen

Google is now the primary action on the account screen rather than a strip above the
email form. They were presented as two equal buttons in a column, which made the better
option look like the fallback: one tap and nothing to remember, against a form. The
button is full width with the four-colour mark, the form sits below a divider that names
it as the alternative, and each has its own error line - sharing one meant whichever
wrote first hid the other.

renderGoogleButton was handed the whole slot, and it empties whatever it is given before
calling Google's renderer. A failed render therefore took the fallback with it, leaving
an empty 56px gap - in exactly the case the fallback exists for. It now gets its own
child to render into, and the styled button stays on screen underneath.

Fixed two strings that were not Vietnamese: "Đã tạo mối" and "Đã chào mối" both mean a
romantic partner. They now say they created an account.

Note on the deployed bundle: nong-trai-two.vercel.app is still serving
index-SVsC98df.js, byte-identical to the previous build, so the environment variables
still have not reached a production build. This commit adds the screen; it does not fix
that.
